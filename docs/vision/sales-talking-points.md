## The five-minute version

### 1. The shift
AI shouldn't be a tool your company *uses*. It should be the operating system your company *runs on*. Every workflow, every decision, every artifact flows through an intelligence layer that learns and improves on its own. We call that an **autonomous company** — and the gap in the market is that no one has shipped the operating system to run one.

### 2. Closed loops
- *Open loops* (most companies today): make decision → execute → no systematic feedback. Information is lossy.
- *Closed loops* (autonomous companies): every action produces an artifact the central intelligence learns from. The next decision is materially smarter.
- This is the math that compounds. Productivity gains are linear. Closed loops compound exponentially.

### 3. The two halves
A closed-loop company needs both:
- **Memory** that makes the company *queryable* (the brain)
- **Agents** that make the company *executable* (the hands)
Single-agent tools and chatbots ship one half. We ship both, designed as one system, with shared identity and shared knowledge graph.

### 4. Shared knowledge is the wedge
A team of agents sharing one memory is qualitatively different from a fleet of agents with siloed contexts. The Sales agent knows what Strategy decided yesterday. The Legal agent knows what Sales agreed to last week. The CEO agent sees all of it. **No re-explaining. No silos. No drift.**

### 5. Bring your own model
The brain is yours to choose. Claude Sonnet 4.6 is the default; swap in GPT, Gemini, Llama, Mistral, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Memory and agents are model-agnostic, so you optimize cost and capability independently — without rebuilding your stack.
