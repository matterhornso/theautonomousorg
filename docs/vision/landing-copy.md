### Section 1 — The problem

**Eyebrow:** Why this matters

**H2:** Your meetings are your most valuable artifact. You forget 90% of them by Friday.

**Body:**
Notes drift. Action items get buried in DMs. Decisions you made three weeks ago are now reverse-engineered from Slack archaeology. Your team relays the same context to ten different people. Your AI tools don't know any of it.

This is the open loop problem — and it's the single biggest reason most companies' AI investments stall.

---

### Section 2 — What Memory does

**Eyebrow:** The persistent brain

**H2:** Every meeting becomes structured knowledge.

**Body:**
Record a meeting. We transcribe it (Deepgram). Claude extracts the entities — people, conversations, commitments, events, notes. They're written into a MongoDB knowledge graph that links across every recording you've ever made.

Need to prep for tomorrow's board meeting? Ask Memory. It queries the graph, synthesizes with Claude, and hands you a brief structured around what *actually* matters to this audience: prior commitments, open threads, sensitivities, last conversation.

**Pre-meeting prep that used to take an EA two hours is now thirty seconds.**

### Section 3 — Why it's not just another notetaker

**Eyebrow:** Limitless + Rewind are consumer tools. This is boardroom-grade.

**Three pillars:**

| Pillar | Body |
|---|---|
| **Structured, not transcribed** | A pile of transcripts is not memory. We extract entities, link them across recordings, and let you query the graph — not search a wall of text. |

| **Shared with your agents** | This is the *same* memory your Autonomous agents read from. Memory is not a silo — it's the brain your workforce runs on. |
